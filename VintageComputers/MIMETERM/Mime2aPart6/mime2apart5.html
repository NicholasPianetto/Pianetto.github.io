MIME 2A Terminal [Part 6] Memory Faults, Analog Driver Board

If you recall from my previous posts, one of the terminal CRT chasis is missing the analog driver board. The chasis assembly is sold by Zenith, and the analog driver board we are concerned with has Zenith part number EIA 151-8-1. Initially, I figured I would have to clone this PCB. Not something that would be out of my reach, but something that would no doubt have taken a long time to do not to mention the parts that I would have to source, like the neck connector. I've removed it from one of my terminals and have pictured it here:

I began looking up any sorts of identification and found a potential lead: the PCB has on the component side of the PCB a label: EIA 151-8-1. Googling this yields only one relevant result, this being a fairly sketchy manual hosting site that has a Brunswick service manual. This manual contains the same chasis assembly that is used in the Mime 2A terminals, and has the exact same board pictured. I decided to call the Brunswick number as I was not able to find a single listing on eBay, and to my surprise they actually had the part in stock.
