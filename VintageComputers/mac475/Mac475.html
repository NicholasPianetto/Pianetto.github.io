Macintosh LC 475

Pretty cool computer, I bought this years ago for $40. Has a Motorola 68040 and I also have a PDS networking card in it. Here's the motherboard, I was in the middle of removing the old and leaking electrolyitic capacitors and replacing them. I use this machine primarily for transferring QuickTake 150 camera photos to my modern computer. Maybe I'll try to make an adapter that can convert it portablly onto an SD card, I think that would be very useful.
