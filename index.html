Welcome to my website!

My name is Nicholas Pianetto. Sometimes I go by Nick. I am a senior studying Computer Engineerning at Iowa State. I have worked at Ag Leader in the past as an Electrical Embedded Hardware intern and I am working at Vermeer this summer as an Electrical Engineer Intern. Last spring (2026) I was a TA for Circuits II (EE2300) at Iowa State. Here is my resume.

This site will continue to get updates. I intend to use this site as a simple blog post for my various interests, like vintage computing, computing history, and embedded hardware projects that I may be working on. Other things that I find interesting may be posted here like musical related stuff. I am not active on any social platforms, if you want to contact me please find me on my LinkedIn account.

I do not intend on making this website more "modern" with bloated frameworks. I think the simplicity of plain text HTML is good enough.
