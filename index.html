Wecome to my website!

My name is Nicholas Pianetto. Sometimes I go by Nick Pianetto. I am a senior studying Computer Engineerning at Iowa State. I have worked at Ag Leader in the past as an Electrical Embedded Hardware intern and will be working at Vermeer this summer as an Electrical Engineer. I have much experience developing embedded hardware and I enjoy creating digital logic circuits, though in my free time this summer I may focus on more analog circuits. Hobbies I enjoy are tinkering with vintage computers and like playing guitar.

This site will continue to get updates. I intend to use this site as a simple blog post for my various interests, like vintage computing, computing history, and embedded hardware projects that I may be working on. Other things that I find interesting may be posted here like musical related stuff. I am not active on any social platforms, if you want to contact me please find me on my LinkedIn account.

I do not intend on making this website more modern. I believe plain text HTML is good enough, and I like the BBS design that this website is written to emulate. I think it is fitting given my interest in this era of computing.
